# Style: 3b1b

Animated math in the manner of 3Blue1Brown: an almost black background, thin colored strokes that draw themselves, and formulas set in the typography of math textbooks, written glyph by glyph. It serves to explain mathematical, scientific or technical ideas that gain from a graph or a formula. Kit: `styles/3b1b.js`. Solved example (standing waves on a string, 9 shots, explanatory and with the animation principles): `styles/examples/3b1b.html`.

The kit includes MathJax 3.2.2 (Apache 2.0) in its last two lines, about 2.3 MB, so the HTML stays a single file that works without a network. Read only up to the line that starts with `const MJ_SRC`. Paste the kit with the shell, not the editor: `sed -i '/KIT-START/r <skill folder>/styles/3b1b.js' <slug>.html`.

## FORMAT

```js
expo: 1, post: { flicker: 0, grain: 0, vignette: 0, bg: '#0E0E11' },
ink: [14, 14, 17], light: [236, 236, 236], paper: 0, shadow: null,
```

## Rhythm

- Shots of 2.5–6 s. Music at 80 bpm (a grid of 0.375 s).
- Each thing comes in at its own moment and stays still for a while before the next: writing a formula takes 0.8–1.2 s, drawing a curve 0.8–1.2 s and growing the axes 0.6–0.8 s.
- The whole video is usually one scene that keeps filling up: use `sharedShot` with a shared set and cut only to change the idea. Between ideas, a hard cut to the new scene.

## Color

Background `C.bg`, text and formulas in `C.white`, axes in `C.axis` and grid in `C.grid`. Accents: `C.blue`, `C.yellow`, `C.green`, `C.red`, `C.teal`, `C.purple` and `C.gold`. **Each color is a meaning, and it keeps that meaning for the whole video**: if the curve is blue, the `f(x)` in the formula is blue too; if the tangent is yellow, the `f'(x)` is yellow too. This way the color ties the figure to its formula without arrows or legends. At most three accents per shot.

## Material

Flat vector, no texture, shadow, shine or gradients: uniform-width lines and smooth fills on a smooth background.

- `axes({x, y, w, h, xr, yr, step})` creates a coordinate system with `X(v)`, `Y(v)` and `pt(a, b)`. `drawGrid(ctx, A, a)` paints the faint blue grid and `drawAxes(ctx, A, u)` grows the axes from the origin, with ticks and arrowheads.
- `plot(A, f, a, b)` gives the points of a graph and `strokeOn(ctx, pts, u, {col, w})` draws it (`w`: 7 for curves, 5–6 for auxiliary lines).
- `dot(ctx, x, y, {r, col, s})` draws a point; for it to appear, `s = popIn(lapse(T, t, .3))`.
- `morph(resample(a), resample(b), u)` turns one shape into another: both are resampled to the same number of points and interpolated.

## Movement

- `expo: 1`: everything moves continuously, with no held poses.
- What moves does so with `eio`: it starts and stops smoothly. What grows uses `eo`.
- A point that travels along a curve drags what depends on it (tangent, label, values) in the same frame.
- The camera nearly still: zoom from 1.0 to 1.04 per shot, or a slow pan toward what matters.

## Text

All text goes through MathJax so it has the same typography as the formulas.

- In `build()`: `tex('\\B{f(x)} = \\frac{x^3}{6} - x', {size})` bakes a formula and `txt('slope', {size, col})` bakes text. `size` is the height of 1 em in world px: 100–120 for main formulas and 60–80 for labels.
- The macros `\B`, `\Y`, `\G`, `\R`, `\T` and `\P` paint a part of the formula with the accent of the same name. The accents, ñ, ¿ and ¡ inside `\text{}` and `txt()` are converted automatically.
- In `draw`: `drawTex(ctx, X, x, y, {u, a, align})` writes it. With `u` from 0 to 1, each glyph draws its outline and then fills, from left to right. `texSwap(ctx, A, B, x, y, u)` swaps one formula for another in the same place.
- A label names something once, next to it, and goes out when what it names moves to where it was; after that the color keeps the link.

## Sound

- Music: `MUSIC = { bpm: 80, key: 'D', mode: 'major', prog: [1, 6, 4, 5], inst: { pad: 'strings', bass: 'bass', keys: 'piano', lead: 'piano', perc: null } }`. For a serious or mysterious theme, `mode: 'minor'` and `prog: [1, 6, 3, 7]`. Low energy: 0.25–0.5.
- **Sync**, always soft, below the music:
  - A formula or axes that begin to be written: a note of `piano` from the chord that is playing (`chordAt`), with `v` between 0.25 and 0.35.
  - A curve being drawn or a point traveling along something: a low `whoosh` of the same duration, with `v` between 0.08 and 0.12.
  - A point that appears: `pop` with `f` around 600 and `v` 0.35.
  - A line that grows: `tick` with `v` 0.3.
- Ambience `studio`.

## Review

- The background is smooth and almost black, with no grain, vignette, shine or shadows.
- Each figure has the same color as its symbol in the formulas, and the shot uses at most three accents.
- In captures in sequence, the formulas are written glyph by glyph (outline and then fill) and the curves are drawn from one end.
- The formulas read in full, at 100 px or more, without touching the edge or the graph.
- No label covers a curve in any capture.
